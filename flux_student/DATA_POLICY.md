# DATA POLICY — flux_student

## 1. Prompt-Only Teacher Data
- All synthetic character training assets in this pipeline must be generated strictly from textual prompts using open-source teacher text-to-image models (`stabilityai/sd-turbo` or `black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell`).
- Web scraping of arbitrary uncurated internet images, scraping unauthorized user directories, or extracting weights as images is strictly forbidden.
- No artificial or stub files: all entries in `manifest.jsonl` represent genuine image files generated by the teacher and verified on disk.

## 2. Multiresolution Pyramids
- Generated teacher images (native 256x256) are saved in `data/raw/` and resampled to multi-resolution pyramids:
  - `32x32`: Nearest-neighbor downsampling to preserve sharp retro pixel grid contours.
  - `64x64`: Nearest-neighbor downsampling for the primary neural training resolution.
  - `128x128`: Lanczos resampling.
  - `256x256`: Original teacher output.

## 3. Strict Content Policy & Religious Figure Prohibitions
Zero-tolerance exclusion policy is strictly enforced in code (`student/filter.py`) during prompt generation, data collection, and inference:
1. No representation, depiction, or personification of Allah (God / Divine).
2. No depiction of Islamic prophets or messengers (Muhammad, Isa/Jesus, Musa/Moses, Ibrahim/Abraham, Nuh/Noah, Adam, etc.).
3. No depiction of the Sahaba (Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, Ali, etc.).
4. No personification or embodiment of sacred Islamic symbols or places (Kaaba, Holy Quran, etc.).
Prompts matching prohibited patterns are automatically rejected before generation.
